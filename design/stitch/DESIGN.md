## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of raw, remote trans-Himalayan exploration tempered by world-class precision, institutional safety, and operational calm. Tailored for experienced international adventure travelers (demographic 40+) venturing into Tajikistan, the Wakhan Corridor, and the Pamir Highway, the interface strikes a delicate equilibrium: visceral grit and topographical authenticity alongside reassuring, high-tier logistical transparency.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Tactile Expedition Utility**:
- **Utilitarian Elegance**: Heavy reliance on deep, slate-stone surfaces, atmospheric sand whites, and razor-sharp structural alignments evoking topographical survey sheets and expedition field logs.
- **Reassuring Solidity**: Sturdy, structured components convey mechanical reliability and institutional safety—eliminating travel friction for high-net-worth riders demanding logistical competence (mechanic backup, satellite communications, sweep 4x4 vehicles).
- **Tactile Expedition Markers**: High-contrast technical data badges, route altimetry profiles, and clear operational status indicators evoke the telemetry of premium dual-sport cockpits and field instrumentation.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the geological extremes of the Pamir massif: glacial river beds, metamorphic slate, mineral terracotta dust, and the ultra-thin air of high-altitude skies.

- **Primary Dark (Slate Obsidian / Mountain Stone - `#181F24`)**: Acts as the dominant anchor for structural frames, high-contrast headings, navigation rails, and technical hero zones. It projects stability, mechanical integrity, and timeless luxury. Deep foundation tone extends to `#12171B` for dark surfaces and navigation overlays.
- **Secondary (Pamir Ochre / Terracotta Dust - `#D96B27`)**: The action color, reminiscent of windblown silt along the Panj River and dry single-track passes. Reserved for high-value interactions, journey milestones, selected interactive states, and active expedition alerts. Supported by `#C35619` for focused hover states.
- **Tertiary (Deep Pamir Sky - `#2A4356`)**: Reflects high-altitude horizons (3,500m+) and glacial meltwater. Used for supportive navigational structures, informational chips, and contextual logistical indicators. Paired with `#3B5D75` for soft visual groupings.
- **Warm Surface & Sand (`#F7F6F3`, `#EFECE6`, `#FFFFFF`)**: An organic, warm substrate that eliminates sterile digital blues, creating an editorial, printed-journal quality that reduces eye fatigue for 40+ readers.
- **Text & Hierarchy**: Slate Charcoal (`#1A2024`) delivers maximum WCAG AAA legibility against sand backings; Muted Slate (`#5A6570`) handles secondary metrics and telemetry data; Light on Dark (`#F4F6F8`) ensures crisp, unmistakable readability on obsidian modules.

## Typography

The typographical architecture juxtaposes the bold, sculpted geometric character of **Syne** with the supreme, open-geometry legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headings (Syne)**: Uncompromising, chiseled, and architectural. The uppercase tracking on headlines conveys deliberate expedition grit while retaining an editorial, collector-grade quality.
- **Reading Comfort for Experienced Riders (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: The target demographic (40+) demands instant clarity under varied outdoor and mobile lighting conditions. Body text never drops below 16px, utilizing a generous 1.6 to 1.65 line-height ratio to preserve effortless scanning of critical itinerary details, medical clearance requirements, and altitude acclimation notes.
- **Technical Badges & Micro-Telemetry (`label-caps`)**: Always uppercase with an expanded `0.12em` letter spacing. This treatment establishes a crisp, military-logistical cadence for altitudes, road surface splits, and vehicle telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined **12-column adaptive fluid grid** that mirrors expedition route planning: structured, measured, and uncrowded.

- **Desktop (12 Columns, max width 1360px)**: Columns breathe with `2.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins of `5rem`. Visual compositions split cleanly into 7/5 or 8/4 configurations—pairing dramatic panoramic landscape captures with detailed technical expedition manifests.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1024px)**: Outer margins tighten to `3rem`, with `1.5rem` gutters. Dual-pane inspection cards collapse cleanly into alternating vertical sequences.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px)**: Fluid full-bleed layouts anchored by safe `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Micro-spacing ensures interactive elements maintain a minimum 48px touch target for tactile operation on the road.
- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Layout distance tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) strictly dictate empty breathing space between interactive blocks, ensuring no metric card or elevation chart feels claustrophobic.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid synthetic, overly-digital visual metaphors, depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** combined with **Low-Contrast Geological Outlines**:

- **Layering Principle**: Elevation is primarily established by moving from the warm base tone (`#F7F6F3`) up to crisp container surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), or within dark expedition blocks from `#12171B` up to `#181F24`.
- **Architectural Outlines**: All interactive cards, spec matrices, and badges utilize subtle, crisp borders (`1px solid rgba(24, 31, 36, 0.08)` on light mode, and `1px solid rgba(244, 246, 248, 0.12)` on dark mode). This reflects mechanical hardware tolerances.
- **Expedition Shadows**: When floating elements (modals, fixed navigation bars, floating booking trays) require separation, depth uses ultra-diffused, cool-tinted shadows:
  - Default: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 31, 36, 0.06)`
  - Raised (Active Cards / Modals): `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(24, 31, 36, 0.12)`
- Backdrop surfaces on navigation bars utilize an 85% opacity frost blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over topographical map layers.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft / Precision Engineered** shape language (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Structural Components**: Cards, itinerary blocks, specification tables, and input containers employ an exact `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This evokes machined aluminum panniers, expedition trunks, and field compass housings.
- **Containers & Modals (`rounded-lg`)**: Defined at `0.5rem` (8px) for larger nested containers, maintaining structural discipline without feeling overly organic or toy-like.
- **Functional Exceptions (Pill Badges)**: Status tags, technical indicators (e.g., altitude, road surfaces), and the central Dual-Path CTA switchers deliberately break into full-radius pills (`9999px`) to contrast against the sharp architectural frames, drawing the eye directly to critical trip telemetry.

## Components

### Dual-Path Primary Mode Switcher
The primary landing and navigation anchor features a prominent segmental toggle allowing instant switching between:
- **Guided Expeditions** (Full support, sweep truck, lead guide, mechanic, lodging arranged).
- **Motorcycle & 4x4 Rental** (Self-guided, satellite tracking, custom GPS routes, fleet hire).
Styled as an expedition cockpit instrument: an obsidian foundation (`#181F24`) housing two pill-shaped triggers. The active trigger transitions smoothly into Pamir Ochre (`#D96B27`) with sharp white text; the inactive option rests in muted gray-white typography.

### Badges & Technical Telemetry Chips
High-altitude expedition indicators built for fast scanning:
- **Altitude Metric Badge**: Pill shape, Slate Sky surface (`#2A4356`), pure white text, featuring a mountain contour icon and peak altitude (`4,655m Ak-Baital Pass`).
- **Terrain Ratio Chip**: Off-white substrate (`#EFECE6`), slate border, displaying road conditions (`Gravel 40% / Asphalt 60%`).
- **Support Matrix Chip**: Light terracotta tinted background (`rgba(217, 107, 39, 0.12)`), ochre text (`#C35619`), reading `Support 4x4 & Luggage Included`.

### Tour Preview Cards
Structured as a rigorous expedition log:
- **Header**: High-impact editorial photography framed with an inset altitude gradient.
- **Telemetry Bar**: Immediate 4-metric strip displaying Duration (e.g., `14 Days`), Difficulty Level (`Level 4/5 - Demanding`), Peak Elevation (`4,655m`), and Fleet Options (`KTM 890 Adv R / Toyota Hilux 4x4`).
- **Body & Inclusions**: Clear list with custom checkmark icons confirming support sweep vehicle, local satellite emergency beacon, English-speaking mechanic, and twin-share / single yurt yurt accommodations.
- **Footer**: Pricing stated transparently with all-inclusive tiering and an unmissable button.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button**: Pamir Ochre fill (`#D96B27`), Slate Obsidian typography on dark or White typography on light, 4px corner radius, bold label tracking (`label-md`). Hover state transitions to Deep Ochre (`#C35619`).
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp obsidian border (`1.5px solid #181F24`), transparent background, slate text. On hover, fills solid Slate Obsidian with stark white text.
- **Safety / Emergency Secondary**: Deep Pamir Sky (`#2A4356`) with white text, utilized for satellite SOS reassurance and medical protocols.

### Security, Fleet & Trust Credentials
Dedicated endorsement ribbons emphasizing:
- **Satellite Emergency Response**: Garmin inReach / Iridium satellite network tracking accompanying every convoy.
- **Mechanical Guarantee**: Certified field technician and complete spare parts roster carried in the sweep 4x4.
- **Government Border Permits**: Wakhan Corridor & GBAO Special Permit processing handled end-to-end.
Displayed using high-contrast slate monoline iconography framed by warm sand containers.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
Designed for high visibility and error-free operation:
- Generous padding (`space-md`), 16px minimum text size to prevent iOS zoom issues.
- Clear, floating uppercase field labels (`label-caps`).
- Slate Obsidian focused border (`2px solid #181F24`) with an ochre highlight ring for active date ranges.
